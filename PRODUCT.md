# Product: Higgins Hardwood Flooring website

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), images in `img/`, hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Source:** Muse brief, 2026-10-20; Facebook page meta (facebook.com/HigginsHardwoodFlooring, fetched 2026-10-20)

## Users
- Homeowners (and likely landlords; inferred) in Philadelphia, New Jersey, Delaware County PA and Delaware with wood floors that need sanding, a buff and coat, repairs or replacing.
- They find the link on Facebook, on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- The owner of Higgins Hardwood Flooring, who receives the site for free and should recognise his business in it at once.

## Purpose
Give the business a place to send people: who they are, what they do, where, and an easy way to ask for the free estimate the owner is advertising.

## Positioning
A local floor man restarting his own company and asking neighbours for work: "I'm starting my own wood flooring company back up and I'm offering Free estimates in the area." Personal, local, no-pressure; small jobs welcome ("or just a simple sanding, buffing and coat").

## Capabilities and constraints
- Services (owner's words): replacing or repairing wood floors; sanding, buffing and coat. Facebook category: "Hardwood Floors Refinishing & Installation".
- Area (owner's words): "Philadelphia, jersey, Delco, and Delaware."
- Contact: **Facebook only.** No phone, no email published. Messenger link `m.me/HigginsHardwoodFlooring` resolves to the page (checked 2026-10-20).
- Offer: free estimates. No prices published.
- Unknown: owner's name, hours, years in business, insurance, base town.

## Brand commitments
- Logo (Facebook profile image): gold (#ba933b sampled) serif "H" over "HIGGINS HARDWOOD & FLOORING" in Art-Deco capitals, on deep navy (#0f132e sampled).
- Voice: first person singular ("I'm starting my own..."), neighbourly ("Hey neighbors", "Thanks for supporting a local business").

## Evidence on hand
- Logo only. No work photos, no before/afters, no reviews.
- Facebook page: 97 followers (meta, 2026-10-20).
- The site's two photos are generated examples of a finish (gpt-image-1), not the owner's jobs.

## Principles
1. Phone first; the first screen shows who, what, where and the estimate button.
2. Only facts from the brief and the owner's page; no invented reviews, prices, years or credentials.
3. One contact path that really works today (Messenger), ready to switch to call and text the moment a phone number is known (`CONFIG.PHONE`).
